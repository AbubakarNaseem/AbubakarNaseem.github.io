Experience Behind the Strategy

From Product Research to Creative Strategy

My work in eCommerce started with product research and Shopify execution and expanded into customer research, positioning, landing-page thinking, paid-social creative, CRO, Meta advertising, and development.

That background matters because eCommerce growth is not isolated to one channel. Product, audience, offer, messaging, page experience, creative, and advertising all influence the buying decision.

Today, I combine research-led strategy with hands-on execution, supported by broader eCommerce capabilities across creative, advertising, conversion, and Shopify.
